# Production readiness and improvement queue

Readiness has five explicit categories: availability (20 points), deployment safety (15), recovery (20), security (20) and observability (20), for a maximum of 95. Every rule exposes its weight, observed state, evidence, investigation and priority. Passing rules earn their stated points. Missing, stale, failing or inapplicable observations earn zero; missing evidence makes the assessment provisional. Scores are assessment aids, not a certification or guarantee. The former five-point rollback rule was removed because there is no observation that can verify a retained rollback release. It no longer forces every assessment to remain provisional. Historical scores used a different maximum and should not be compared as raw point totals.

Fresh runtime/health means less than three minutes old. Independent watcher and resource observations must also be recent. Backup evidence must be within the configured RPO (24 hours by default); restore evidence must be health-verified within 30 days. The dashboard presents failed/unverified rules as a prioritized improvement queue within each property. A deployment notification alone does not prove a retained rollback image exists.
